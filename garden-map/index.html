About this map ↓
Geospatial structure

Where the gardens actually are

The circle-packing chart shows the GreenThumb network as a hierarchy — borough, jurisdiction, footprint. This map answers a different question: where are these 635 gardens on the ground? Each dot is one garden, positioned by its latitude and longitude, coloured by borough to echo the packing chart, and sized by footprint so the larger gardens read at a glance.

Click any borough in the legend to isolate it; click a garden for its jurisdiction, size, status, and address. The two objects share a single dataset, so the map and the chart always tell the same story from two angles.

Built with MapLibre GL (the token-free, open-source equivalent of the course's Mapbox tutorial) on a Protomaps vector base. Data: NYC Open Data — GreenThumb Garden Info.

NYC Community Garden Map

Each dot is one of the 635 gardens in the NYC GreenThumb program, placed at its real coordinates and coloured by borough. Dot size grows with the garden's footprint in square metres.

Toggle boroughs on and off from the legend, and click any garden to see its managing jurisdiction, size, status, and street address.
