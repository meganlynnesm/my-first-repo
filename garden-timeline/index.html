Over Time

When are New York's community gardens open? Radar clocks of Wednesday, Friday & Saturday hours, by borough

View chart ↓
Temporal structure

The week in open hours

Each radar reads a day as a circle. The axes are the hours, from 8 in the morning round to 7 in the evening, and each coloured shape is a borough, reaching outward at the hours when most of that borough's gardens are open. Three clocks sit side by side, one for each day the dataset records, so you can compare how the rhythm shifts from Wednesday to Friday to Saturday. Reading time around a circle is what makes it a temporal structure. Built with Nadieh Bremer's radar-chart redesign.

Real data. Every value comes straight from the NYC Open Data GreenThumb records: each garden's listed opening hours for that day. For each hour we count how many of a borough's gardens are open, as a share of that borough's gardens that list hours that day, so the shapes stay comparable across boroughs and days. (Staten Island lists hours for only a handful of gardens, so its shape is coarse; the count under each clock is honest about the sample.)

Chart by Nadieh Bremer (D3.js). Data: NYC Open Data GreenThumb Garden Info (Wednesday / Friday / Saturday open hours).

Three days, one clock each. Each axis is an hour; a borough's shape reaches out where more of its gardens are open that day. Hover a point for the exact share. Data: NYC Open Data GreenThumb open hours (Wed / Fri / Sat).
