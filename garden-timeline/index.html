Over Time

When did New York's community gardens take root? A time series of the GreenThumb network, year by year

View chart ↓
Temporal structure

Growth of the garden network

The plan for this object is a time series of how many NYC community gardens were established each year — read as a cumulative curve, the story of a grassroots movement taking hold across the boroughs from the 1970s vacant-lot era onward. The technique follows the D3 time-series approach from the stat679 notes (a d3.scaleTime x-axis with d3.line / d3.area path generators).

This is a blank canvas for now. The garden dataset behind the map and circle-packing chart doesn't carry a founding year, so the chart below is scaffolded — scales, axes, and a ready renderTimeline() function — but waiting on the year data before it can draw.

Built with D3.js. Data pending: garden founding / establishment years.
